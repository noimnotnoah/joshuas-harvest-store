Our Story

Nestled on Gosford Road in the heart of Ottery St Mary, Joshua's Harvest Store is a family-run gourmet country store celebrating the finest West Country food. We carefully source seasonal produce, artisan cheeses, cured meats, freshly baked breads, and an exceptional selection of wines and craft ales from Devon and beyond.

Whether you're planning a countryside picnic, a Sunday roast, or simply looking for that perfect local gift, our shelves are stocked with care and provenance.
